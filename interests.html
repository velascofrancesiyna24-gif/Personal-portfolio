THE THINGS I ENJOY

My Interests

Everyone has different things they enjoy doing, and I also have hobbies that make me happy and allow me to express myself. In my free time, I enjoy doing different things that make me happy. I like playing instruments, dancing, singing, cooking sometimes, and drawing or painting.I enjoy doing different activities, learning new things, and spending my free time doing what I love.

Playing Instruments

I enjoy playing different musical instruments, such as the drums, guitar, bass guitar, and keyboard. I use these skills to worship the Lord as part of our church music team. For me, playing instruments is not only a hobby but also a way to serve God through music.

Dancing

I enjoy dancing because it became a hobby that my cousin and I started during the pandemic. We had fun dancing together, and over time, I learned to enjoy it even more. Until now, dancing is still one of the activities I love doing.

Singing

I enjoy singing, especially when I get to use my voice to glorify God as a backup singer in our church. For me, singing is not just something I enjoy doing, but also a way to worship and serve the Lord. It makes me happy knowing that I can use my voice for Him.

Cooking

I started enjoying cooking when I was young because my Lola used to cook for us. As I grew older, I also learned to love cooking, and it became one of my hobbies. Until now, I still enjoy cooking, and I am now the one who usually prepares meals for our family at home.

Drawing and Painting

Drawing and painting also became my hobbies, especially when I have nothing else to do. I enjoy doing them because I want to learn more and improve my skills. I also like trying different ideas and seeing how much I can improve over time.

Badminton

I started enjoying badminton when I was in elementary school. I used to join competitions and enjoyed playing the sport. Until now, I still enjoy playing badminton, and I also had the chance to play during our school intramurals. It became one of my favorite memories and an activity that I will always remember.
