MY LEARNING JOURNEY

My Education

My education is an important part of my life because it helps me learn new things and grow as a person. In every school I have attended, I have gained new knowledge, experiences, and lessons that helped shape who I am today. As I continue my studies, I hope to learn more, improve myself, and reach my goals in the future.

01. Daycare

I started my learning journey at a daycare in Bocaue, Bulacan. It was one of my first experiences in school, where I began learning new things and meeting people. Until now, my former teacher and I still know each other which makes this part of my childhood more special and memorable.

02. Elementary

During my elementary years, I studied at three different schools: Balagtas Central School in Bulacan, Sta. Lucia Elementary School in Capas, Tarlac, and San Juan de Mata Elementary School in Tarlac City, where I continued my elementary education. My family and I moved from one place to another for certain reasons, which led me to experience studying in different schools. Even though I had to adjust to new places and people, each school became part of my learning journey and helped shape the person I am today.

03. Junior High School

I studied at Tarlac National High School during my junior high school years. I joined the Special Program in Foreign Language (SPFL), where I learned how to speak Mandarin. Through this program, I also had the chance to join different events and meet Chinese people, which gave me opportunities to practice speaking Mandarin and learn more about their language and culture.

04. Senior High School

I continued my studies at Tarlac National High School for my senior high school years, where I chose the STEM strand. During this time, I had more opportunities to grow as a person, develop my skills, and become more confident. I also got the chance to join different school organizations and participate in events, which helped me gain new experiences and learn more about myself.

05. College

I am currently studying at Tarlac Agricultural University, taking up Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT). In this course, I am learning about computers, programming, and technology. I also hope to develop my skills, gain new knowledge, and grow as a person. Through my college journey, I want to prepare for my future career and be able to help and support my family someday.
